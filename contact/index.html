1. Receive your message
We'll process all messages and typically respond within 24-48 hours.

2. Review and respond
Our team will carefully review your feedback and provide a helpful response.

3. Take action
If it's a suggestion, we'll consider it for future development. If it's a bug, we'll investigate and fix it.

💡 Tips for Your Message

  • Be clear and specific about what you want to communicate
  • Include your contact information (we'll use your email)

  • For feature requests, explain how it would improve your experience
  • Be patient - we'll get back to you as soon as possible
