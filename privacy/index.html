Privacy Policy

At FreeToolsZone, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

🛡️ Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use our tools. All tools operate directly in your browser, and any text or data you input is not stored on our servers. This means your information stays completely private and secure.

📊 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain non-personal information may be collected automatically. This may include:

  • Your IP address

  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent

This data is used only to improve website performance, understand user behavior, and enhance your experience. We do not use this data for any other purpose or share it with third parties.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand user behavior and optimize our website for better performance. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use our tools.

Types of cookies we may use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

📺 Advertising and Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements through third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads based on your interests and browsing history.

We do not control how third-party advertisers use your data. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information on how they handle your information.

Our security measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

Our website may contain links to external websites and third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of these third-party sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately before providing any information.

If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information and notify the parents or guardians immediately.
