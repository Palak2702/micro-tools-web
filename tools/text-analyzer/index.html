    Advanced Text Analyzer – Complete Writing Analysis Tool

    The advanced text analyzer provides detailed insights into your content, including word count, unique words, paragraphs, reading time, speaking time, and more. It is designed for writers, bloggers, and SEO professionals.

    What is a Text Analyzer?

    A text analyzer evaluates your content and provides multiple metrics to help improve readability, structure, and engagement.

    What You Can Analyze

    • Total words and unique words
    • Character count
    • Sentence and paragraph structure
    • Reading time
    • Speaking time
    • Estimated pages

    Why Use a Text Analyzer?

    Understanding your content helps you improve writing quality and SEO performance. It allows you to optimize structure, readability, and engagement.

    Reading Time vs Speaking Time

    Reading speed is faster than speaking. This tool calculates both, helping you plan scripts, presentations, and content length effectively.

    How to Improve Content Using Data

    • Use shorter paragraphs
    • Maintain consistent sentence length
    • Avoid repeating words
    • Optimize reading time
