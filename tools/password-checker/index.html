Why Password Strength Matters

Millions of accounts are hacked every year due to weak passwords. Simple passwords like "123456" or "password" can be cracked in seconds using automated tools.

A strong password can take years or even centuries to crack.

Using a password strength checker helps you identify weaknesses and improve your password security instantly.

What Makes a Strong Password?

  • At least 12–16 characters long
  • Includes uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Contains numbers and symbols
  • Avoids common words and patterns
  • Unique for each account

How Hackers Crack Passwords

Hackers use methods like brute force attacks, dictionary attacks, and phishing to gain access to accounts. A weak password can be cracked in seconds, while a strong one can resist these attacks for years.

Tips to Create a Secure Password

  • Use a password manager
  • Never reuse passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Use long and complex phrases
