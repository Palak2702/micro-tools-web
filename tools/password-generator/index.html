What is a Password Generator?

A password generator is an online tool that helps you create strong, random, and secure passwords instantly. Instead of using weak or predictable passwords, this tool generates complex combinations of letters, numbers, and symbols to improve your online security. Strong passwords are essential for protecting your accounts from unauthorized access and cyber threats.

Why Are Strong Passwords Important?

Weak passwords are one of the main reasons for account hacking and data breaches. Using simple passwords like "123456" or "password" makes your accounts vulnerable. A strong password includes a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, making it difficult to guess or crack.
