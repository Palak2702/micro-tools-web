Ideal Word Count for SEO in 2026

Word count plays an important role in search engine optimization. While quality matters more than quantity, longer content often performs better in search rankings because it covers topics in depth.

  • Blog posts: 1200–2000 words
  • SEO articles: 1500–2500 words
  • Landing pages: 500–800 words
  • Product descriptions: 300–600 words
  • Social media posts: 50–300 words

Using a word counter ensures your content stays within these recommended limits.

How Many Words Per Page?

The number of words per page depends on formatting and writing style. On average:

  • Typed (A4, 12pt font): 250–300 words
  • Single spaced: 500 words per page
  • Handwritten: 150–200 words

This tool helps you estimate pages automatically, which is useful for assignments, reports, and books.

Word Count for Different Platforms

Different platforms require different content lengths. Here are some practical guidelines:

  • Instagram captions: 100–150 words
  • YouTube scripts: 1000–1500 words for 8–10 minute video
  • LinkedIn posts: 150–300 words
  • Twitter (X): 280 characters (~50–60 words)
  • Blog introductions: 100–200 words
