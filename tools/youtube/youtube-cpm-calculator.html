What is YouTube CPM? (Meaning)

CPM (Cost Per Mille) means how much advertisers pay per 1000 views. Simply: CPM = money earned per 1000 views (before YouTube cut)

CPM to Earnings Example

If CPM = ₹100
1000 views = ₹100
100,000 views = ₹10,000

YouTube CPM by Country

  • India: ₹40–₹300
  • USA: ₹500–₹2000
  • UK: ₹400–₹1500

How to Increase YouTube CPM

  • Choose finance/business niche
  • Target US audience
  • Improve watch time
  • Create longer videos

CPM ka matlab kya hota hai? (Hindi)

CPM ka matlab hota hai advertiser 1000 views ke liye kitna paisa deta hai. Agar CPM ₹100 hai, toh 1000 views par ₹100 milenge (YouTube cut se pehle).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CPM per video or per month?

CPM per 1000 views hota hai, not per video or per month.

Why is CPM low in India?

India me advertiser budget kam hota hai compared to US.
