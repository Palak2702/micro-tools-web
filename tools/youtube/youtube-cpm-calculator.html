YouTube CPM Meaning (Full Explanation)

YouTube CPM (Cost Per Mille) means the amount advertisers pay for 1,000 ad impressions on your videos.

In simple terms, CPM shows how much money your content generates before YouTube takes its share.

Many creators search for “YouTube CPM meaning” or “YouTube CPM full form”, but the real understanding comes from how it affects your actual earnings.

Real Example

If your CPM is ₹120 and your video gets 10,000 views:

10,000 ÷ 1000 = 10
10 × ₹120 = ₹1200 (before YouTube cut)

But this is not your real income. Not all views show ads and YouTube takes around 45%.

YouTube RPM vs CPM (Important Difference)

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is your actual earning after YouTube’s cut and monetized views.

This is why your real income is always lower than CPM.

YouTube CPM in India (2026 Updated)

YouTube CPM in India is lower than countries like the US or UK because advertisers spend less per user.

  • India: ₹40 – ₹300
  • USA: ₹500 – ₹2000
  • UK: ₹400 – ₹1500

However, CPM depends heavily on niche:

  • Finance / Business: ₹250 – ₹600+
  • Tech / Education: ₹120 – ₹300
  • Entertainment / Gaming: ₹40 – ₹150
