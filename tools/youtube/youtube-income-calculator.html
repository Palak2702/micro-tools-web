What factors affect your YouTube income in India

Your YouTube income in India is not fixed. It changes based on multiple factors related to your content and audience, and performance.

  • Content category – Finance and tech videos usually have higher CPM than entertainment. However, entertainment content can get more views and go viral, which can increase total income.
  • Audience location – Views from countries like the US pay more than India
  • Watch time – Longer watch time increases ad revenue
  • Engagement – Likes, comments, and shares improve performance
  • Video type – Long videos earn more than Shorts
  • Returning viewers – More repeat viewers help grow your channel
