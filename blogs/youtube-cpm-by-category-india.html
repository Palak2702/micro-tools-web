What Does CPM by Category Mean?

CPM is not fixed across YouTube. It changes based on what your content is about.

Advertisers pay more in categories where users are more likely to spend money — like finance, software, or business.

That’s why a finance video can earn multiple times more than an entertainment video with the same views.

YouTube CPM by Category in India (2026 Updated)

Here’s a realistic breakdown of CPM rates in India:

  • Finance / Investing: ₹250 – ₹600+
  • Business / Online Earning: ₹200 – ₹500
  • Technology / Software: ₹120 – ₹300
  • Education / Tutorials: ₹100 – ₹250
  • Health / Fitness: ₹80 – ₹200
  • Travel / Lifestyle: ₹70 – ₹180
  • Food / Cooking: ₹60 – ₹150
  • Vlogs: ₹40 – ₹120
  • Gaming: ₹30 – ₹100
  • Entertainment / Comedy: ₹40 – ₹120

These values are not fixed, but they reflect what most creators experience in India.

Highest CPM YouTube Categories

If your goal is maximum earnings, these categories consistently perform better:

  • Finance and stock market
  • Business and entrepreneurship
  • Software tools and SaaS

These niches attract high-paying advertisers because viewers are more likely to spend money.

Low CPM Categories (Reality Check)

Some categories get massive views but lower earnings:

  • Entertainment and viral content
  • Gaming (especially non-English)
  • Daily vlogs

These niches rely on volume, not value. You need significantly more views to match high-CPM niches.

Real Example (Why Category Matters)

Let’s say two videos get 100,000 views:

  • Finance video (CPM ₹300) → ₹30,000 (before cut)
  • Entertainment video (CPM ₹80) → ₹8,000 (before cut)

Same views, but nearly 4x difference in earnings.

How to Choose the Right Category

Don’t just chase high CPM blindly. Choose a niche where:

  • You can consistently create content
  • You understand the topic
  • There is advertiser demand

A balanced approach works best — content you can sustain + decent CPM.

Finance, business, and software-related content usually have the highest CPM.
