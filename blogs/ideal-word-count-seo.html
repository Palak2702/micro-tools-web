Ideal Word Count by Content Type

  • Blog posts: 1200–2000 words
  • SEO articles: 1500–2500 words
  • Landing pages: 500–1000 words
  • Product pages: 300–600 words

Does Word Count Affect SEO Rankings?

Word count itself is not a direct ranking factor, but longer content tends to rank better because it covers topics more comprehensively.

High-quality long-form content increases dwell time, backlinks, and search visibility.
